## Codebase Patterns
(This section will be updated as we learn patterns during development)

- Flask server runs on port 5001
- WebSocket events use flask-socketio
- Puck firmware uses PlatformIO with Arduino framework
- MPU6050 gyroscope Z-axis for spin detection (rad/s * 57.2958 = deg/s)
- FastLED for WS2812B LED ring (16 LEDs on GPIO 23)
- ESP-NOW for puck-to-puck communication (250m range)
- TV game templates go in server/templates/tv_games/
- Routes for TV games follow pattern: @tv_games_bp.route('/tv/<game-name>/<int:puck_id>')
- Three.js v0.158.0 with UnrealBloomPass for 3D games
- Use _TEMPLATE_3D.html as starting point for new 3D games

## 2026-01-09 - US-003 COMPLETE: Dramatic Wheel Physics
Thread: https://ampcode.com/threads/$AMP_CURRENT_THREAD_ID
- **Achievement**: Transformed basic wheel into casino-quality experience
- **Files changed:**
  - server/templates/tv_games/shot_roulette_royale.html (major physics upgrade)
- **Implementation details:**
  - Cubic ease-out deceleration curve (matches backend physics)
  - Slow-motion effect for final 2 seconds (lerpFactor 0.02-0.1)
  - Web Audio API: whoosh on spin start, clicks on zone crossings
  - Click frequency increases as spin slows (600-1000 Hz for tension)
  - Zone highlighting: pulsing during slow-motion, bright on landing
  - Brief flash (0.5 emissive) when crossing each zone
  - "SO CLOSE" indicator when within 18° (5%) of adjacent zone
  - Spin duration calculated: 3-8 seconds based on velocity
- **Learnings for future iterations:**
  - Web Audio must be initialized on user interaction (first spin)
  - Cubic ease-out (1 - (1-p)³) feels much more realistic than linear lerp
  - Slow-motion finale is CRITICAL for tension - reduces lerp to 0.02
  - Zone crossing detection via modulo arithmetic and currentAngle tracking
  - setTimeout for brief flash effects works well without blocking animation
  - Pulsing emissive intensity during slow-motion builds anticipation
  - "SO CLOSE" indicator adds dramatic near-miss moments
